Strategy rules

How the fund works

Initial allocation
14 green stocks on 17 November 2026, €1,000 per stock.

Daily purchases
A newly green stock triggers a €1,000 position. Existing cash is used first; only a shortfall is funded externally.

Quarterly sales reviews
30 January, 30 April, 30 July and 30 October. Sell only when a confirmed latest model is neither green nor blue-yellow.

Benchmarks
SPY and URTH buy and hold. They receive only the same external capital flows as the fund.

Cash
Proceeds and dividends remain in cash until the next purchase; 0% interest assumed.
