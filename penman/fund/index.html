About Rakkejak Fund

Rakkejak Fund is a hypothetical, rules-based portfolio informed by the accounting-based valuation work of Professor Stephen H. Penman. Growth alone is not enough: capital must produce an attractive return to create value. The analysis tracks growth in net operating assets (NOA), return of NOPAT on net operating assets (RNOA), rate of reinvestment and the return on incremental capital.

On 17 November 2026, the paper portfolio began with 14 green-rated positions of €1,000 each. A new verified green signal can trigger a €1,000 buy after the next eligible completed market close. Available cash is used before new external simulated funding.

Sales are considered only at quarterly reviews on 30 January, 30 April, 30 July and 30 October. You can watch our watchlist via the Valuation Monitor.

Buy disciplineVerified new green signal, next eligible market close, €1,000 per position.
Quarterly sale reviewNo sale unless the latest verified classification is neither green nor blue-yellow.
